Title: How Sharon Online lessons work

Summary: The lesson format, joining on time, the lesson memo, cancellations and keeping students' information private.

## Body (markdown)

### The lesson
Every lesson is a 25-minute, one-to-one video lesson with a student. It takes place in our in-browser classroom: you open
your lesson from your dashboard and join from the same page. You do not need to install an app or share a personal link.

### Before each lesson
- Check your internet and audio. You told us your speed and power backup when you applied; keep them true.
- Join a few minutes early. The student is waiting for you.
- Use a quiet room, a clear microphone and a plain background.

### Attendance
We record when you and the student join and leave the classroom. A lesson counts as delivered when the recorded time in the
room is long enough. If you do not join, the lesson can be marked as a tutor no-show and the student is refunded.
If the system cannot tell what happened, the lesson goes to staff review. You are never marked absent only because of a
technical fault on our side.

### The lesson memo
After each lesson write a short memo for the student: what you covered, vocabulary, and what to practise next. Submit it
within the deadline shown on your dashboard (you receive a reminder; a missed memo deadline can affect that lesson's
payment).

### Cancelling or rescheduling
- Cancel as early as you can. Cancelling at least 24 hours ahead carries no penalty (provisional).
- Late cancellations count as strikes, and repeated strikes can lead to your account being paused (provisional).
- If you have a power cut or connection failure, tell us straight away through your dashboard.

### Privacy
- Your private notes about a student are visible to staff only. Never share a student's contact details or move them
  outside the platform.
- Students' written reviews are private to staff.
